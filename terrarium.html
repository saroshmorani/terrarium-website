🌱 Creating Your Self-Sustaining System

Understand the steps for ecological engineering at the micro-scale.

1. Choose Your Vessel 🏺

Select a clear, sealable container to serve as the dome for your micro-environment. This establishes the closed system for moisture recycling.

2. Add Drainage 💧

Layer materials like pebbles and activated charcoal to manage water flow and prevent root asphyxiation, mimicking natural subsurface drainage.

3. Soil & Substrate 🌍

Introduce the appropriate nutrient rich soil mix—the living medium that supports plant growth and contains decomposers.

4. Plant Your Greens 🌲

Carefully place small, humidity loving plants (like mosses or ferns) that thrive in the adapted climate, emphasizing species selection.

5. Fine Tune & Decorate ✨

Arrange plants and add natural elements like stones or branches, completing the habitat structure and niche distribution.

6. Water & Seal 🔒

Mist lightly to introduce the initial moisture, then seal the container to begin the self regulating water cycle and ecological balance.

A simple step towards understanding global systems and ecological balance.
